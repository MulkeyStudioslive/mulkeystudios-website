FAQ & Requirements

Know what to expect before you apply.

Mulkey Studios is built for creators who want to learn, communicate, and improve their LIVE presence.

Application Requirements

Who should apply?

Good fit

  • Creators with LIVE access
  • Creators willing to learn and communicate
  • Creators who want to improve their stream
  • Creators open to onboarding and resources
  • Creators focused on long-term community

Not a good fit

  • Anyone expecting guaranteed followers, gifts, or income
  • Anyone unwilling to communicate
  • Creators looking for shortcuts only
  • Creators not interested in going LIVE consistently
  • Anyone currently violating platform rules

FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need LIVE access?

Yes. LIVE access is required. TikTok checks eligibility through the official application process before Mulkey Studios can send an invite through Backstage.

Does joining guarantee followers, viewers, gifts, or income?

No. Mulkey Studios provides training, resources, community, and support. Results depend on the creator’s effort, consistency, content, audience, and platform performance.

Does it cost money to apply?

No. Mulkey Studios does not charge creators to submit an application.

Do I have to be a gamer?

No. Mulkey Studios is built around three main creator pathways: gaming, battle, and chat.

Do I have to battle?

No. Battles can be useful for some creators, but Mulkey Studios is not built around forcing every creator into battles.

Do I have to collaborate with other creators?

No. Community and networking are available, but creators are not required to collab, battle, or work with other LIVE hosts.

What happens after I apply?

You apply through the official TikTok link first. TikTok checks eligibility. If you are eligible, Mulkey Studios can send the official invite through Backstage and then provide onboarding steps.

Is acceptance guaranteed?

No. Applying does not guarantee eligibility, an invite, followers, viewers, gifts, income, or acceptance. TikTok checks eligibility first, and Mulkey Studios reviews creators individually.

Is Mulkey Studios independent?

Mulkey Studios is a creator support organization. This website does not claim sponsorship, employment, endorsement, or representation by any third-party platform.

Ready to apply?

If you have LIVE access and want support built around growth, submit your application.
