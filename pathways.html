Creator Pathways

Simple paths for the main types of LIVE creators.

Mulkey Studios focuses on three clear creator pathways: gaming, battle, and chat. These cover the main types of creators we support without making the system feel complicated.

The Three Pathways

Choose the path that fits how you go LIVE.

Gaming Creators

For creators who build LIVE content around gameplay, retro games, speedruns, challenges, or gaming communities.

  • Stream setup and presentation
  • Talking while playing
  • Building a repeat gaming audience
  • Game choice, challenges, and content ideas
  • Short-form content pulled from LIVE streams

Battle Creators

For creators who use battles, rankings, gifting goals, teams, and collaboration-based LIVE formats.

  • Battle consistency and scheduling
  • Supporter community building
  • Battle communication and etiquette
  • Understanding what drives viewer support
  • Keeping supporters engaged without burning out

Chat Creators

For creators who grow through conversation, personality, engagement rooms, panels, hosting, and community interaction.

  • Conversation flow and topic planning
  • Audience engagement
  • Building regular viewers
  • Running lives without dead air
  • Creating a space people want to return to

No Forced Format

You do not have to battle or collab to belong here.

Some creators grow through battles. Others grow through games or conversation. Mulkey Studios keeps the pathways simple while respecting that not every creator should follow the same strategy.
