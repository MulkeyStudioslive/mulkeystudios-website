About Mulkey Studios

A creator network built around development.

Mulkey Studios is a creator network dedicated to helping TikTok LIVE creators grow through education, resources, community, and support.

We believe creators deserve more than being treated like numbers in a system. Every application is reviewed individually, and accepted creators receive access to onboarding, guidance, and resources designed to help them improve.

What We Offer

Support built for real creators.

Creator Training

Onboarding videos, creator guides, and practical training for TikTok LIVE growth.

Growth Resources

Resources focused on consistency, engagement, retention, and building a stronger community.

Creator Community

A private Discord space for accepted creators to learn, connect, and grow together.

Technical Support

Guidance for stream setup, audio, cameras, OBS, internet, and basic troubleshooting.

Creator Pathways

Different creators need different guidance.

Gaming Battle Creators Chat Hosts Music Creative & Hobby Educational

Mulkey Studios is designed to support multiple creator styles. Whether you are entertaining, educating, gaming, battling, performing, or building a community around a hobby, our goal is to help you find the path that fits your content.
